Delete your account

Hanguk Student App · operated by Hanguk (hanguk.uz)

This page explains how to request deletion of your Hanguk Student App account and the data associated with it.

Option A — Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open the Hanguk Student App.
  2. Go to the Account screen (from the home tab settings / account icon).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm and re-authenticate.

Your account and the data listed below are removed as described.

Option B — Request by email

If you cannot access the app, email privacy@hanguk.uz from the email address or phone number associated with your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We action verified requests within 30 days.

What data is deleted, and what is kept

Delete some data without deleting your account

To remove specific items (for example a document or an interview session) without closing your account, delete them in the app, or email privacy@hanguk.uz describing what you want removed.

Questions about your data? Contact privacy@hanguk.uz. See also our Privacy Policy.
