# Play Store Feature Graphic

**Required size:** 1024 × 500 PNG (no alpha).

The feature graphic appears at the top of your Play Store listing on
phone and Play Store web. Apple has no direct equivalent.

## Content guidance

- Lead with the app icon at the left, large and clear.
- Wordmark "Hanguk" in the brand vibrant lime on the dark blue gradient
  background.
- A subtle Korean motif (e.g. taegeuk-inspired curve, Seoul skyline
  silhouette) at the right.
- No phone bezels, no device frames — Google rejects feature graphics
  that look like screenshots.
- No marketing copy embedded in the image — Google strips it for
  some surfaces.

## File naming

Save the final PNG as `hanguk_feature_graphic_1024x500.png` in this
directory. The Play Console upload form accepts a single file.

## Localized variants

If the wordmark differs by locale (Hangul vs Latin), produce one
graphic per supported locale and name them:

- `hanguk_feature_graphic_en_1024x500.png`
- `hanguk_feature_graphic_ko_1024x500.png`
- `hanguk_feature_graphic_uz_1024x500.png`

Upload each in the corresponding "Store listing translations" tab.
